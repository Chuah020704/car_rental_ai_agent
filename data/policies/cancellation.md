# Cancellation Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15_

Reservations may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled pickup time, with a full refund processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. Cancellations made within 48 hours of pickup incur a cancellation fee equal to one day's rental rate, deducted from the refund.

Cancellations within 6 hours of the scheduled pickup time, or no-shows, are non-refundable. Reservations with Full Insurance Protection purchased follow the same cancellation windows, but the insurance fee itself is always fully refunded regardless of timing. All cancellations must be confirmed by the customer before being processed — our AI assistant will always ask for explicit confirmation before cancelling a reservation on your behalf.
