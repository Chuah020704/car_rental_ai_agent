# Fuel Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15_

All vehicles are provided with a full tank and must be returned with a full tank. Customers who return a vehicle with less fuel than provided will be charged for the missing fuel at the prevailing pump price plus a RM 30 refuelling service fee. Receipts from refuelling shortly before return are not required but may be requested if a fuel discrepancy is disputed.

Diesel vehicles (vans and the Toyota Fortuner) must only be refuelled with diesel; misfuelling with petrol is not covered by insurance or roadside assistance, and the customer is fully liable for resulting engine damage and recovery costs.
