# Roadside Assistance

_Last updated: 2026-01-15_

Every rental includes complimentary 24-hour roadside assistance covering flat tyres, battery jump-starts, lockouts, and towing to the nearest authorized service center within Peninsular Malaysia and Langkawi. Call the roadside assistance hotline printed on your rental agreement; average response time is 45-60 minutes in urban areas (KL, Penang, JB) and up to 90 minutes in Langkawi.

Roadside assistance does not cover fuel delivery for running out of petrol/diesel (a RM 50 callout fee applies for fuel delivery), damage caused by driving through flooded areas, or breakdowns resulting from customer negligence such as ignoring a dashboard warning light. If a breakdown is due to a vehicle defect rather than customer action, no charges apply and a replacement vehicle will be arranged at no cost.
