# Insurance Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15_

All rentals include Basic Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) covering damage to the rented vehicle up to RM 2,000 excess, which the customer is liable for in the event of an incident. Customers may purchase Full Insurance Protection at checkout to reduce this excess to RM 0 and add Third-Party Liability coverage up to RM 1,000,000. Full Insurance Protection costs an additional RM 25 per day and must be purchased before pickup; it cannot be added retroactively after an incident has occurred.

Insurance does not cover damage caused by driving under the influence, off-road use outside designated tracks, or use by a driver not listed on the rental agreement. All incidents, regardless of insurance coverage, must be reported to our 24-hour support line within 2 hours of occurrence to remain valid.
