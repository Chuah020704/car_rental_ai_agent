# Age Requirements

_Last updated: 2026-01-15_

The minimum age to rent any vehicle is 21 years, with a valid driving licence held for at least 1 year. Drivers aged 21-24 are subject to a Young Driver Surcharge of RM 20 per day, which does not apply from age 25 onward. Premium category vehicles (Mercedes-Benz, BMW) require the primary driver to be at least 25 years old regardless of licence duration.

International visitors must hold a valid International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside their home driving licence for the full rental duration. All drivers must be listed on the rental agreement and present their licence at pickup; unlisted drivers are not covered by insurance or roadside assistance in the event of an incident.
