# Damage Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15_

Vehicles are inspected and photographed at pickup and return. Any new damage identified at return that was not noted at pickup will be assessed against the vehicle condition report, and repair costs will be charged up to the customer's insurance excess (RM 2,000 under Basic CDW, RM 0 under Full Insurance Protection). Minor wear such as small scratches under 2cm or stone chips is not chargeable.

Customers must report any damage or mechanical issues immediately via our 24-hour support line; continuing to drive a vehicle with a known safety issue may void insurance coverage for any resulting incident. Total loss or major structural damage is assessed at the vehicle's market value at the time of the incident, less any applicable insurance coverage.
